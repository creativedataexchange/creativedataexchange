About

Creative Data Exchange explores how emerging technologies are reshaping creative practice across disciplines.

It creates space for open, accessible conversations about data and AI — grounded in lived creative experiences.

Who is it for?

Artists, researchers, and creative practitioners with curiosity about data and technology, regardless of experience level.
