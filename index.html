About Creative Data Exchange

An antidote to AI hype. A provocation for change. A space where technology is demystified through collaborative conversation.

Creative Data Exchange is an initiative presented by the Contemporary Narratives Lab, in partnership with Goldsmiths Futures of Creativity Institute and Fuel Theatre Company. Supported by a 2026 Software Sustainability Institute Fellowship.

Art Data AI Ethics
Driving Question

How can data and AI develop creative practice — and conversely, how can creative practice shape the development of data and AI technologies?

What We Are Asking

Two-hour conversations for experienced creative practitioners with little to no prior experience using data.

✉

Facilitation

Structured dialogues led by researchers and practitioners at the intersection of data, AI and the arts.

👥

Why Are They Facilitating These Conversations?

There is a digital divide that excludes many artists from vital debates on copyright, visibility and funding in an age of algorithmic transformation.

These conversations are motivated by a desire to bridge that divide — to create a space where artists, researchers and technologists can meet on equal ground, ask difficult questions, and imagine better systems together.
